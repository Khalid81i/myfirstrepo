**Date checked:** Tuesday 10 November 2026. Every advert below was opened individually on that date.

## How to read this

- **Availability is advertised status only.** No agent was contacted, so nothing here is agent-confirmed. "Available" means the advert was live and not marked let agreed when opened.
- **Walking times are routed, not estimated.** Each is a pedestrian route from the advert's map pin *and* from its full postcode to the station entrance, run through two independent OpenStreetMap routers (OSRM foot and Valhalla pedestrian). The range shown is the lowest to highest of those results. A property is in Section A only if the *highest* figure is 15 minutes or less. Google Maps was not used, and the route starts at the building's pin or postcode, not a specific flat door, so allow a minute or two either way.
- **Train times** come from the National Rail journey planner for Wednesday 11 November 2026 (a normal weekday), both directions.
- **Bills:** no qualifying one-bed flat includes bills. Rent excludes council tax, electricity, gas, water and broadband in every Section A row. Council tax band is given where the advert states it.
- **Single occupancy:** all Section A adverts are whole flats let to one household; none excludes a single tenant.
