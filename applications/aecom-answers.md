## Renewable Energy
My passion for renewable energy comes from wanting to use my electrical engineering knowledge to help solve one of the biggest challenges facing the world: producing reliable, affordable energy while reducing environmental impact.

During my Electrical and Electronic Engineering degree, I particularly enjoyed power systems and energy transfer. My dissertation on double-sided LCL-compensated capacitive power transfer strengthened my interest in efficient energy systems and gave me valuable experience in MATLAB modelling, simulation and technical problem-solving.

What attracts me to AECOM is the opportunity to contribute to real renewable energy projects, particularly solar PV, wind power and battery energy storage. I’m especially interested in experiencing the complete engineering process, from feasibility studies and electrical design through to construction and commissioning.

AECOM’s commitment to sustainable infrastructure, combined with its graduate development programme and opportunities to learn from experienced engineers, makes this an ideal place to begin my career and contribute to the transition towards cleaner energy.

My dissertation involved developing MATLAB simulations to analyse a complex power transfer system, strengthening my analytical skills and understanding of how different components interact.

I’ve also gained valuable teamwork and leadership experience, including leading a university project where several team members were unavailable. By reorganising responsibilities and maintaining communication, we successfully achieved a first-class result.

AECOM’s SESA graduate role appeals to me because it combines technical problem-solving with systems integration, verification, validation and safety assurance. I’m particularly excited about working alongside different engineering disciplines, learning how complex railway projects are delivered and developing the expertise to contribute to safer, more reliable transport infrastructure.
